# 📌 Mustapha Gbanie — Official Autobiography Website

🌿 **My Life. My Journey. My Story.**
*A digital record of where I started, the challenges I faced, the lessons I learned, and the dreams I am still fighting to achieve.*

---

## 📖 About This Project
This is my personal biography website, built to share my true life story with the world. It documents my journey from my birthplace in **Tungie, Gorama Mende Chiefdom, Kenema District — Sierra Leone**, through my education, my discovery of technology, and my struggle to learn and build web solutions entirely using only a mobile phone 📱💻.

It highlights my mission: to prove that background and lack of resources are never limitations — passion, faith, and hard work are what truly define success. It also serves as an inspiration to other young people who have big dreams but limited tools or support.

**Live Preview:** https://mustapha-official.github.io/Autobiography_Mustapha_Gbanie/

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
I built this entire project myself, from scratch, right on my mobile device, using the following technologies:

- **HTML5** — For the structure and layout of all pages
- **CSS3** — For styling, colors, responsiveness, and beautiful design
- **JavaScript** — For interactive features, slider/carousel sections, and mobile navigation menu
- **Font Awesome** — For beautiful icons used throughout the site
- **Google Fonts** — For clean, modern, and readable typography
- **Swiper.js** — For the smooth sliding effect on *Inspiration Notes* and *People Ask* sections

### ✍️ Special Note:
- **Development & Coding:** 100% hand-coded and built by **Mustapha Gbanie**. Every line of code, every design choice, and every feature was created and implemented by me.
- **Grammar & Language Support:** I was assisted by **Dola** — who helped review, polish, and correct grammar and phrasing in the written content and story notes, ensuring the biography reads clearly, professionally, and correctly — while keeping every detail and message exactly as I intended it to be.
