His Full Life Story

The journey from humble beginnings to where he is today — every step, every lesson, every dream.

Origins

This is the story of Mustapha Gbanie — a young, determined man born with a vision far greater than his circumstances.

He was born on 20th May 2004, in the peaceful village of Tungie, Gorama Mende Chiefdom, into a loving and supportive family. His father, Kekura M Gbanie, and his mother, Jeneba Gbanie, raised him with care, strong values, and the hope that he would one day grow to be someone great. Raised in a humble, middle-class home in Kenema District, Sierra Leone, Mustapha received his entire education right here — from his very first days in primary school, all the way through secondary level until he successfully sat for and completed his WASSCE examinations.

From a young age, Mustapha had a clear, burning purpose in his heart: he wanted to change his life, and lift his entire family from their current standard of living to a much better, higher, and more secure future. This goal was so important to him that he made a brave and wise choice early on: he decided to stay away from romantic relationships or dating. He understood well that such things could bring distractions, early responsibilities, and heavy burdens he was not yet ready to carry. He feared adding stress to his life while his parents — especially his father — were still working hard to support him. So, he chose to focus entirely on his studies, his growth, and building his future, trusting that hard work and patience would take him exactly where he wanted to go.

Realization and focus

As he grew older and began to watch how the world was changing, Mustapha realized one powerful truth: Technology is the path to real, big success. He saw that the future belongs to those who master digital skills, and he knew instantly: this is where I belong. This is what I must learn.
He took his first step by enrolling in computer classes, starting from the very basics. When he sat his exams for Introduction to Windows and Microsoft Word, he achieved excellent grades — a result that filled him with confidence and proved to him that he truly had the mind and the ability for this field. He planned to go even further, learning PowerPoint and other advanced programs, but at that time, his father encouraged him to pursue higher education instead — and Mustapha listened, knowing his father’s guidance was wise.

He gained admission into the Eastern Technical University of Sierra Leone, first studying BSc Social Work. After completing his first year, his father asked him to change his course. By now, Mustapha’s heart was already fully captured by technology; he deeply wanted to study ICT, because he knew exactly what he wanted to become: a Professional Web Developer.

Challenges

However, there was a big challenge: the fees for ICT courses were very high — much more than his family could easily afford. Mustapha worried deeply about the burden this would place on his father, but his father’s words gave him strength: “Just go, Allah is the one who provides everything.” Trusting in those words and in God’s plan, Mustapha enrolled — and from that moment, he began his own serious journey into the world of coding and web development.
He started learning HTML, and quickly moved to CSS. He understood them well, loved every part of it, and soaked up knowledge like a sponge. But here is the most amazing and inspiring part of his story: Mustapha Gbanie learns, codes, and builds everything entirely on a simple mobile phone — an Itel A56. To this day, he has never owned or used a computer or laptop.

His journey has been far from easy. His phone is old and slow. Internet connection is often weak and unstable — at times it was so difficult that he had to pause his learning of advanced languages like JavaScript and PHP for a while. But did he give up? Never. He kept going. He used every free minute he had to practice, to read, and to build, he's really a true legend.
At university, he realized that the school did not offer the specific courses he truly wanted, nor did they have the proper tools or computers for practical work. In fact, he soon discovered that he was learning far more from online tutorials and his own late-night research at home than he ever did in class. Still, he respected his father’s wish and attended his classes faithfully — but every spare moment on campus, and every quiet hour at night while others slept, Mustapha was busy: coding, studying, testing, and growing his skills. That is how he mastered HTML and CSS — through pure determination, even without the right tools.

Purpose, Vision & Legacy

Today, he is already proud of how far he has come. One of his biggest dreams has already come true: his name and his work are visible on Google Search, known to people all over the world. Have you seen his other works? search "Mustapha Gbanie" to discover more of his works. Now, he has started his own small business: building websites for local shop owners and businesses, helping them establish their presence online, grow their reach, and succeed too.
His journey is still ongoing. He is the first to say honestly: “I have not yet been fully successful in life; I am still struggling, still fighting to become someone great one day.” Every day, he hopes and prays for the opportunity that will take him higher — because he knows he has the talent, the mind, and the will to do great things. Yet, through every difficulty, he says “Alhamdulillah” — Praise be to God — because he knows he is approaching something much bigger, something greater than he can even imagine now.

His goals are simple but huge: to become one of the greatest developers, to create solutions that help people all over the world, to build a name that is respected everywhere, and to leave a legacy that will never fade.

Closing Words

Mustapha Gbanie — a son of Kenema, a fighter, a dreamer, a self-made developer. Always learning. Always building. Always moving forward. And one day, the whole world will know his name as a man who started with nothing but a phone and a dream, and rose to greatness.
