Questions People Often Ask Him

About His Life & Journey

Where are you from?

He was born in Tungie, Gorama Mende Chiefdom, Kenema District, Sierra Leone.

What was your childhood like?

Simple, humble, full of curiosity and big dreams, raised with strong values from his parents.

How did you start learning technology?

Self-taught entirely, started with basic computer classes then learned everything online using only a mobile phone.

What challenges have you faced?

Lack of resources, slow internet, no mentor, financial struggles — but he turned every difficulty into strength.

What are your future plans?

To become globally recognized, build solutions, help young people, and make Sierra Leone proud worldwide.

Are you in a relationship?

Regarding romantic relationships, he prefers to keep his personal life private. His belief is: "When you love what you do and work hard for it, that is where true purpose lies."
